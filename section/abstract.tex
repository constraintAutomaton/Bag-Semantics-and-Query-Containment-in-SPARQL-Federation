\begin{abstract}
Querying federations of distributed data sources lets applications integrate heterogeneous datasets without centralizing them, but places significant computational load on both federation endpoints and querying clients.
In settings such as scientific reproducibility studies, queries are frequently re-executed or iteratively refined, compounding this cost.
Query containment is a classical mechanism enabling a range of techniques to optimize queries.
Query containment for federated SPARQL queries, however, remains unexplored.
This paper closes this gap by giving a decision procedure for query containment under bag-set semantics for federated SPARQL queries with \texttt{SERVICE} clauses.
We additionally cover \aclp{UCFQ}, a fragment strictly between \aclp{CQ} and \aclp{UCQ} that captures the queries arising under automatic source selection.
The procedure relies on \acl{BFR}, a transformation from \acl{UCQ} to \acl{CQ} under bag semantics.
\end{abstract}
