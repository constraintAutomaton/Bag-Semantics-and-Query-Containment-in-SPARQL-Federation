\begin{abstract}
Querying federations of distributed data sources lets applications integrate multiple datasets without first having to centralize them,
but comes at the cost of higher execution times and bandwidth usage.
In various use cases such as data analyses, queries are frequently re-executed or iteratively refined, compounding this cost.
Materialized views and query containment form the basis for improving the performance of such query sequences.
Query containment for federated SPARQL queries, however, remains unexplored.
This paper focuses on closing this gap by formalizing query containment under bag-set semantics for federated SPARQL queries,
as SPARQL offers a natural foundation for integrating RDF data via universal identifiers and global semantics.
