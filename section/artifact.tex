\section*{Artifacts}
The executable examples accompanying this paper are publicly available and run on the Comunica~\cite{taelman_iswc_resources_comunica_2018} SPARQL federation engine: bag-set query containment at \url{https://github.com/constraintAutomaton/demo-semantic-query-containment-SPARQL}, and federated query semantics at \url{https://github.com/constraintAutomaton/demo-federated-query-semantic}.
A detailed proof that \texttt{JOIN} cannot raise the multiplicity of a solution mapping in a \ac{CQ} is available at \url{https://github.com/constraintAutomaton/SPARQL-multiplicity-join}.
